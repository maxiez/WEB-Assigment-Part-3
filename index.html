Welcome to CodeMzansi

We change the lives of many, so that you and we can prosper and become a success.

The focus and aim of this website is to educate society about CodeMzansi, a non-profit organisation that travels around Africa to help people with technology and to offer basic skills and training free of charge, as a means of making our continent a better place, day by day.
