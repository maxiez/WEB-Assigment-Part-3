About CodeMzansi

Our story

This website is proudly brought to you by CodeMzansi, a non-profit organisation founded by Max Sekgabi. It was a dream back in 2022, when Max travelled around the continent of Africa and saw the shortage of technology skills and the huge gap this created. Because of globalisation, technology is now at the forefront of today's world and economy, yet many of Africa's youth and teens lack technology and computing skills, which keeps the continent from moving forward. This gave Max a hypothesis: how much would Africa's youth learn and prosper if they had basic computer skills and technology knowledge? He started an organisation determined to teach Africa's brilliant young minds about technology and computing, seeing it as a massive opportunity to also reduce the unemployment rate, which increases every year.

Mission and vision

Our mission is clear and straightforward: to better the lives of young people in the best way possible, and that means education, the best shield an individual can own today. We do this by travelling around Africa, targeting less privileged communities and townships, and going to the youth to teach them about technology. Our team teaches young people the basics of using a computer: the hardware and all its components, how they work and how to fix them, and shortcuts that make life easier for the computer and its user. We also teach people how to make software, to build their credibility, critical thinking and intelligence. See all of our services.
