More information about us

Because of our love for technology and computers, we occasionally travel around our home country, South Africa. We visit local parks and the sides of highways to clean the streets, making our environment clean and beautiful and recycling the trash. We also raise awareness of pollution and how it affects the world and us living in it. We educate the youth about e-waste (any discarded electrical or electronic device), the dangers of e-waste and how to reduce it, because it is becoming a major issue that does not get the attention it deserves.

We urge everybody to stand up and help us make a real change in our world. Please volunteer, bring your family and friends and help us clean the streets of South Africa.
